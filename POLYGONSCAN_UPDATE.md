## Project description (paste)
CHARM is a friendly, fixed-supply ERC-20 token on Polygon built for small acts of appreciation such as tipping, thank-yous and community rewards. The total supply of 1,000,000,000 CHARM was minted once at deployment, with no transfer tax and no minting afterwards. A free faucet lets newcomers claim their first CHARM.
